Track 01 · 4:12

Ferrite Bloom

Ashgrove TrioRidgeway HallTake 7

Recorded on the second night in the old methodist hall, with the piano wheeled against the rear wall and two blankets over the windows. The trio played the head twice and kept the second take, which is why you can hear a chair settle in the last bar.

Track 02 · 3:48

Slow Tape Hiss

Marla VintRidgeway HallTake 2

One microphone, one voice, one afternoon. Marla asked that the hiss be left exactly where it fell rather than trimmed by the engineer, and the master carries it through to the fade. The result is quieter than the session tapes around it and should be played loud.

Track 03 · 5:03

Copper Sunrise

The Ferrite FourStudio B, OsakaTake 4

The only track on this side not cut at Ridgeway. The quartet had four hours booked in Osaka and used three of them on the horn arrangement, then played the finished take at dawn with the blinds up. The count-in is audible on purpose.

Track 04 · 6:21

Nightbus to Ridgeway

Ashgrove TrioRidgeway HallTake 1

Written on the coach home and rehearsed the following morning on the venue piano. Nothing was written down: the form was agreed by counting, and the middle section runs eight bars longer on the tape than in the sheet the label later printed.

Track 05 · 2:57

Room Tone

Marla VintRidgeway HallContinuous take

Two minutes and fifty-seven seconds of the hall with everyone in it and nobody playing: radiators, traffic on Ridgeway Road, a door in the vestry. It closes the side because the room was the sixth member of every session on this record.

F1Sessions

The Ridgeway sessions

Eleven nights in the winter of 1979, booked because the hall was cold, cheap and free after eight. The label paid for the piano tuner, the tea and the fire, and the musicians kept every penny of the door.

All five tapes were cut on quarter-inch reel at fifteen inches per second and transferred in 2024 without noise reduction, in the order they were recorded rather than the order they were pressed.

F3Masters

Tape masters

The originals live in a steel cabinet above the boiler, which is the wrong place for tape and the only place with a dry air supply. Each reel carries a paper leader handwritten with the date, the running order and whoever was in the room.

Digital transfers are made once a year, checked against the reel, and stored in two buildings. If you hold a copy of FR-014, it was pressed from the second transfer, made before the splice on reel three gave way.

F4Info

Visiting the label

The listening room opens on Saturday afternoons with four decks, two pairs of headphones and a shelf of everything the label has pressed since 1980. No booking, no charge, and the kettle is always on.

Trade, licensing and reissue enquiries are answered by post and by telephone, in that order. We keep the catalog number on every sleeve so that quoting it is enough.

Liner notes

Ferrite Records, catalogue FR-24. Side A was assembled from the Ridgeway sessions of 1979 and the Osaka date of the following spring. Every title on this side is a tab on the deck above: the label is the menu.

Produced by the Ferrite Four. Engineered by D. Okonkwo at Ridgeway Hall and by S. Maruyama in Osaka. Tape direction by Marla Vint. Sleeve printed letterpress on 120 gsm in the back room of the shop.

Longer notes, session photographs and the complete running order appear in the four sections below the deck.
