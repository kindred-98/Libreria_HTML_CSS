Appraisals & probate

Our specialists travel with a case of loupe, notebook and light. A verbal appraisal takes twenty minutes; a written report for insurance or probate takes five working days.

  1. 01Visit

    A specialist comes to the house, photographs each piece and notes condition, marks and repairs.

  2. 02Research

    Comparable results from twenty-two years of our own sale records, plus the international auction index.

  3. 03Report

    A signed valuation with figures, provenance notes and a recommendation on whether to sell or keep.

04

The house

Founded by Eleanor Cordell and the bookbinder Arthur Vane in a single room on Endell Street, the firm has outlived two wars, three moves and one very bad fire in the silver store.

Today thirty-one staff work between the saleroom, the warehouse and the cataloguing office. Every lot is measured, weighed and photographed under the same north light that Vane insisted on in 1894, and every catalogue entry is written by the specialist who handled the object.

We do not charge for a first opinion. If a piece is worth less than the cost of selling it, we will say so plainly and suggest a museum, a school or a collector who will care for it better than the market would.
