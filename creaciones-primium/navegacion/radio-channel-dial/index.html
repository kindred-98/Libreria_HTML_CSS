Ridge Lane Radio Society · Volunteer run since 1994

88.1

Noticeboard

Morning Noticeboard

Weekdays 07:00 – 09:00 · Presented by the news desk

The day begins with the notices: road closures, the swimming timetable, two lost bicycles and whatever the bakery has run out of by nine. Readers telephone the studio, and the presenter reads the slip exactly as it is written, spelling mistakes included.

Between the notices there is weather for the valley only, because the valley is where the listeners are, and a one-minute silence for anyone the town has lost that week. Nothing on this channel is scripted more than an hour ahead.

  • 07:00Roads, rails and the school crossing
  • 07:40Valley weather and river levels
  • 08:20Lost, found and for sale
91.5

Playlist

The Long Playlist

Weekdays 18:00 – 21:00 · Presented by Nell Achebe

Three hours, no advertisements, and one rule: every record must have been played in public somewhere in this town within the last month. Requests are taken on a postcard and read out with the sender's street, never their full name.

The playlist keeps a running ledger of what has been played, which is kept in a ring binder behind the desk. Regulars can request a repeat only if the ledger shows they have not been given one since the spring.

  • 18:00Three from the ledger
  • 19:15Postcard requests
  • 20:30The long one nobody asked for
94.7

Workshop

Workshop Hour

Saturdays 11:00 – 12:00 · Live from the repair room

Two microphones, a bench and whatever has been carried in that morning: toasters, lamps, a church bell clapper and, once, a submarine navigation light. Listeners describe the fault, the bench describes the fault back, and half of the repairs are finished on air.

The set that broadcasts this programme was built in the same room in 1994 from a workshop manual and a scrap receiver. Its tuning capacitor still has a dead spot between ninety-four and ninety-five, which is why this channel sits where it does.

  • 11:00First three on the bench
  • 11:30Salvage corner
  • 11:52Safety note and sign-off
98.3

Streets

Council & Streets

Tuesdays 19:00 – 20:00 · Recorded at the civic hall

The council meeting, edited to one hour without cutting a single decision. Where the original runs long, the presenter summarises the debate and then plays the vote in full so that every name is heard exactly once.

Question time is taken from cards left at the library desk during the week. If a question cannot be answered in the meeting, the answer is broadcast the following Tuesday at the same point in the programme.

  • 19:00Planning and the old depot
  • 19:30Questions from the desk
  • 19:50The vote, in full
102.9

Night

Night Signal

Nights 23:00 – 03:00 · Unattended transmission

After the volunteers go home the transmitter holds the frequency with a recorded carrier: slow music, the occasional interval signal, and a spoken clock at the top of every hour for the night workers and the insomniacs who have asked for it.

The programme is deliberately unexciting. Testimony from three night-shift drivers suggests the best thing radio can do at two in the morning is not surprise anyone, and the playlist has not changed much since the winter of 2011.

  • 23:00Opening carrier
  • 01:00Spoken clock and weather
  • 03:00Handover to the noticeboard
106.5

Tape

Tape Archive

Sundays 14:00 – 16:00 · Transferred from quarter-inch

Reels from the society's cupboard, digitised in the order they were found: a fête from 1996, the flood of 2007, the last night of the swimming baths and a demonstration of how to rewire a plug that has been requested more often than anything else in the archive.

Each broadcast is announced with its date, its tape number and whatever the box says in biro. Where the tape has perished, the gap is left in and explained rather than edited out.

  • 14:00Reel 12 · the summer fête
  • 14:45Reel 31 · the flood
  • 15:30Reel 04 · how to rewire a plug
