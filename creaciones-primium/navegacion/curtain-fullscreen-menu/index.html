01 Lineup

Type set
loose

Four days of letters in Valencia: thirty-four speakers, nine workshops, one printing hall that stays open until two in the morning because nobody wants to stop pulling proofs.

The 2026 lineup leans on people who draw for screens and people who cut metal, and refuses to pretend those are different professions. Every talk is followed by a specimen table where the fonts discussed that afternoon can be printed, held and argued with.

02 Talks

Four stages, one clock

Talks run in forty-minute blocks with twenty minutes of overlap, so you can always leave one hall and still catch the opening of the next. The schedule is printed daily on a single broadsheet because the wifi in the old hall has never once cooperated.

03 Workshops

Nine rooms, twelve seats each

Workshops are deliberately small. You bring a laptop or a scalpel, you leave with a printed specimen and a corrected drawing, and the instructors mark the corrections in front of everyone, which is the fastest teaching method ever invented.

W1

Broadside in a day

Composing, locking up and printing a one-sheet poster on a Vandercook. Includes ink under the fingernails.

12 seats
W2

Drawing the counters

Why the holes decide the character. Four hours of curve correction with a glass board and a stubborn tutor.

12 seats
W3

Variable axes from scratch

Setting up two axes, interpolating properly and finding the masters that quietly break at 400 weight.

10 seats
W4

Brush to vector, honestly

Scanning a painted alphabet and keeping the wobble that made it worth painting in the first place.

12 seats

04 Venues

Three buildings, one street

Everything happens inside a four-hundred-metre radius on Calle de Sorní: the lecture halls in the old exchange, the workshops in the print school, and the night programme in a former ticket office with excellent acoustics and terrible chairs.

05 Tickets

One price, no tiers

We do not sell a cheap ticket that turns out to exclude half the talks. There is a full pass, a student pass and a workshop add-on, and every one of them gets into every hall, every night.

06 Archive

Three editions already

Every edition leaves a trace: the printed programme, the corrected specimens, the wall of rejected logotypes. Scans of all three previous programmes hang in the corridor outside Hall C, and the originals travel to a different city each summer.
