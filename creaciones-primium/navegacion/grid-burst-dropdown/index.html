Kessler Industrial Supply · Unit 7, Dock Road

Thirteen aisles, one counter

The catalogue button above is the navigation: pick a department and the entry below changes to match it. Every aisle keeps its own stock count and its own price band, and the ledger at the end totals the whole unit for the quarter.

01 · Fasteners

Bolts that stay put

Hex head to grade 8.8, socket cap to grade 12.9, and a self locking nut that works wet or greasy, which matters more on a roof or in a trench than the tensile figure ever will. Sold by the box of two hundred; the trade counter will not break a box under fifty pieces.

02 · Power tools

Cordless and fixed

Two 18 volt platforms and one 36 volt, with batteries that are swapped rather than charged in the case, because a warm battery in a van is a battery that will not last the winter. Angle grinders come with a side handle and the guard fitted, and we will not sell one without it.

03 · Abrasives

Discs, belts and pads

Cutting discs are 125 millimetres, 1 millimetre thick, rated 13 200 rpm, and the label is the point: an unlabelled disc is not a cheap disc, it is a mystery. Belts come in three grits and pads in two, and the whole department fits on one shelf by the door.

04.1 · Hand tools

Wrenches

Ring spanners in the sizes the trade actually uses, adjustable patterns with a fine knurl for small work, and the stubby variety that fits where a full length one will not. Every wrench is drop forged and laser etched with its size, because a painted size wears off in a year.

04.2 · Hand tools

Pliers and cutters

Combination, side cutting, long nose and a pair of end cutting nippers that will take a 4 mm cable without marking the insulation either side of the cut. The insulated tools are tested to five thousand volts and the certificate travels with the tool, not with the box it came in.

04.3.1 · Hand tools

Measuring gauges

Vernier calipers to 150 millimetres and outside micrometers to 25, both steel rather than plastic, both with a check gauge so you can prove they are still true before you trust them on a part that matters. Digital heads are welcome here and are not sold here.

04.3.2 · Hand tools

Spirit levels

The 600 millimetre aluminium box section with three vials, and a longer 1 200 millimetre for floors and roof line. The vials are replaceable and the box has a milled edge, so a level with a knocked corner still reads true against a straight surface.

05 · Plumbing

Pipe and fittings

Copper tube in coils, compression fittings in brass, and the push fit fittings that have quietly replaced half the fittings we used to sell. If you are fitting under a floor, ask for the barrier versions; if you are fitting inside a chase, the ordinary ones are fine and cheaper.

06 · Electrical

Cable, boxes and glands

Three core and earth, 1.5 and 2.5 square millimetres, on 100 metre drums, with the drum label showing the actual length run and not the nominal. Back boxes come in plaster, metal and the plastic shallow pattern for plasterboard, and the glands are the nickel plated brass type.

07.1 · Adhesives

Tapes and films

Cloth tape for cable bundling, PTFE thread seal tape that goes on dry, and a masking tape that will not lift a coat of paint off a dado rail if it is left overnight. We do not sell gaffer tape as a water pipe repair, whatever the label says on the internet.

07.2 · Adhesives

Gaskets and seals

Graphite sheet cut to size, fibre washers, nitrile O rings in a drawer of sizes, and a sealant that cures slowly enough to align a steel frame and quickly enough to hold pressure by morning. Bring the flange drawing if the bolt pattern is unusual.

08 · Safety

Head, eye and hearing

Site helmets with a ratchet cradle that fits over a hat, safety glasses that pass the impact mark rather than sitting on the shelf, and ear defenders for anyone using a breaker more than twenty minutes a day. All of it is dated, and dated stock goes in the bin.

09 · Storage

Bins, racks and trolleys

Stackable bins in four sizes with clip lids, a boltless rack that assembles with an allen key, and a folding platform trolley for the difference between the yard and the counter. The rack is rated at 300 kilos a shelf pair and the label says so on the upright.
