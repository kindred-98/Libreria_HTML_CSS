01Accounts

The account book

Every engagement is signed against a named partner, so the account plate in the masthead always tells you who answers the phone. Open it, pick a row, and the whole desk re-labels itself: the header, the mobile panel and the running ticker all follow your choice.

  • HA Harrow AthleticBrand, retail graphics and matchday printPartner: Iris Whitcombe · since 2016 Live

    Fourteen fixture programmes a season, from the programme book to the turnstile vinyl. Work is planned in six-week blocks so the print room never sees a same-week surprise.

  • VF Verity Foods GroupPackaging systems and store rolloutPartner: Aurelio Sanz · since 2011 In review

    Eleven regional rollouts share one artwork system. Colour is locked to a press proof book, and every new store is photographed against the same grey card before it ships.

  • NC Northgate ClinicWayfinding and patient informationPartner: Dr Halima Bex · since 2021 Live

    Two hospital sites, 340 rooms and a sign family that must read at forty metres and at arm's length. Contrast is checked against the clinical colour palette, never against taste.

  • PR Pell and RoeIdentity for an architecture practicePartner: Jonas Pell · since 2019 On hold

    Identity, stationery and a drawing-set standard. The work is paused while the practice completes a site survey, and the account stays visible so nobody has to remember which drawer it is in.

  • SR Solstice RailPassenger information and liveryNew business · opened May Proposal

    Late-stage proposal for a four-region operator. If it lands, the account plate becomes the busiest object on the studio floor and the masthead had better keep shrinking well.

03Billing

Invoices, terms and the small print

One statement per account, issued on the first working day of the month and tied to the retainer rather than to individual jobs. Drafts live in the account plate, so the number you see in the masthead is the number your client sees.

Retainers

Five of the seven retainers are quarterly. The two exceptions exist because those clients consolidate payment across regions, and a single monthly statement would put a translator between us and the approver.

48 statements issued this quarter

Scope changes

A change is written up before it is worked. The note names the trigger, the extra days and the person who approved it, then it waits in the account plate until the partner signs it off.

7 change notes, 0 disputed

Press and print

Production costs are passed through at cost with the invoice attached, never marked up. Studios that hide the press invoice lose the client eventually; we have found the honest version works better.

Press invoices attached to 100% of jobs
