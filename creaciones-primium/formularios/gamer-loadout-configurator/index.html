    House rules

    • Slot names take up to 18 characters and no two slots may share the same name.
    • Drag is a whole number of kilograms, capped by the tier ceiling in the table below.
    • Drag for the whole squad may never pass 24 kg. The meter turns red before that happens.
    • Sealing a sheet needs at least five filled slots. Blank rows are skipped by the sums.
