Station 01

Green dome, red roofs

Five hundred metres up, the city has stopped being streets and become a field of angles. One green dome and one clock face are the only things still standing up straight.

Station 02

Main Street, north

Drop a hundred metres and the roofs turn back into a street. A single tram track, a line of painted signs, and a haze so thick that the far end of the canyon is only a rumour.

Unknown author · Public domain

Station 03

Coloured roofs, close up

The last twenty metres are the loudest. Corrugated roofs in five colours, one tree with a proper crown, and a lane too narrow for anything wider than the red vehicle parked in it.

Cookie Nguyen · CC BY-SA 4.0
