Frost almanac · Brocken to Nelson

Winter, day by day

Nine stops hang on one vertical spine. Arrow keys walk the spine, Enter raises the plate, Escape lays it down again.

  1. First rail of the season

    The rack railway takes its first train above the tree line while the ridge still holds the night’s frost.

  2. Low sun, loaded branches

    Two clear days let the sun reach the canopy; every spruce carries a bar of white on its north side.

  3. Drift across the valley

    Wind out of the north-east packs the cutting shut; the only line left open is the one the train owns.

  4. Whiteout in the canyon

    On the other side of the continent the same air arrives as a wall, and the spires go quiet.

  5. The pass at midday

    A short thaw, enough to soften the crust and make every footprint a hard blue shadow.

  6. New year, new drift

    Fresh snow erases the tracks and the ridgelines become the only writing left on the page.

  7. Walls without sound

    Snow swallows the courtyards; the old brickwork reads only as a shadow line on white.

  8. Rock above the trees

    The bare larches let the tower keep its outline; by February it is the only landmark with an edge.

  9. Thaw at the lake

    The season closes with four degrees and a roof losing its load one plank at a time.
