BAY I

Walk 06 / three bays / nine plates

The Nave

One walk, three bays, and a photograph in three depths in each of them. Scroll and the far arcade moves a little, the middle a little more and the near row a good deal further: that difference is the whole building.

I The West Door II The Crossing III The Choir

I

The West Door

Flags at the west end, a painted dome overhead and the far wall of a very different building. Three plates, three distances: the narrow column at the back is the one that barely moves.

II

The Crossing

An altar with a screen of candlesticks, a ruined nave kept exactly as it fell and one painted interior from 1842. The bay is offset to the right so the walk never takes the same line twice.

III

The Choir

Two churches, photographed one after the other on the same grey day. The last plate is the one you leave the building on, and the grid lines run right off the bottom of the page.
