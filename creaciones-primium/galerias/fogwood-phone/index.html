Walk 04

8 stops

Dülmen · Mossel · Synevyr

Stop 01 of 08 · Meadow under fog

  1. 01 Meadow under fog. The grass goes white first, then the spruce, then the hill. Nothing here is sharp and nothing is meant to be.
  2. 02 Black spruce in mist. Spires in a plain, the fog standing higher than the ground it hides.
  3. 03 Road at sunrise, Dülmen. Beams come through sideways and land on the asphalt, which is the only thing that holds an edge.
  4. 04 Canopies against a low sun. Bare crowns, an orange band of sky, and fog gathered in the field below the trunks.
  5. 05 Fog coming down the slope. Seen from above, the fog behaves like a tide with no shore.
  6. 06 Green beech, Mossel. The only stop where the light is green rather than white.
  7. 07 The valley at Synevyr. An autumn path, and above it a lake of cloud filling the whole basin.
  8. 08 Red beech hedge. Copper leaves in the near field, a wall of grey behind them, and the two planes never touch.
