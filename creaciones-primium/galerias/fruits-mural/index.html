Colour market / folio one / fruit, photographed

Everything here was once a seed

Eight spreads, one fruit wall. Scroll at your own speed: the mural keeps its sections, and the ticker between the blocks never stops, so the page is never quite still.

01

The wall, arranged by hand

Somebody stacked all of this in an afternoon: a display where the only rule is that the eye should find a new colour at every step. The photograph takes the whole left half of the spread and bleeds off the edge of the page.

Tolatheart · CC BY-SA 4.0

02

Cut, salted, eaten standing up

Street food, no plate to speak of. This spread runs the other way, so the mural never settles into a single rhythm: the frame hangs on the right and the folio number sits hard against the left margin.

Nami Verma · CC BY-SA 4.0

03

The same wall, a century earlier

Colour photography caught up with the fruit trade eventually. The archive frames are printed to look like the paper they were rescued from, and they run the full width of the page so the mural keeps bleeding instead of stopping at a column.

Fortepan, donor LHM · CC BY-SA 3.0

04

Awning, bananas, a face in the gap

The mural keeps the human frames too. A striped awning, a stack of bananas, a vendor looking straight down the lens: this is the spread where the fruit stops being fruit and turns into a street.

Fortepan, donor Lőw Miklós · CC BY-SA 3.0

05

Close enough to count the seeds

The tightest frame on the page: cut fruit at arm length, the camera close enough that the seeds are the subject. Above it the ticker keeps running, which is the only thing in this mural that refuses to end.

Nenad Stojkovic · CC BY 2.0

06

Before the wall, there was the tree

Every plate so far has been fruit after the fact, picked and stacked. This one is fruit still on the branch, which is the honest place to start a mural about colour.

Chenspec · CC BY-SA 3.0

07

Weighed, crated, priced

There is a scale in this frame and a crate under it, which is the whole commercial argument of a market in one photograph. Grapes on the left, apples on the right, a number in the middle that nobody in the picture can read.

Fortepan, donor Konok Tamás · CC BY-SA 3.0

08

And at the end, dessert

The last spread leaves the market and arrives somewhere sweet: jelly cubes in colour, whipped cream on top, and the ticker finally slowing down as the page runs out of fruit.

epodrez · CC BY 2.0
