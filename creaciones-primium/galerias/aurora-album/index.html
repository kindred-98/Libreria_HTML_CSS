Album 03 · four leaves · eight plates

The Long Night Album

Eight photographs of the northern lights kept on four folded leaves. The album is shut when you arrive; pick a leaf and it opens out to the whole screen, two pages at a time.

Contents

Tab to a leaf and press Enter, or use left and right arrows, to open the album.

Eight photographs from Wikimedia Commons by Chris Danals, the United States Air Force, Rafal Konieczny, Ximonic, Ross Burgener, Kristian Pikner, Jamen Percy and Johannes Groll, released under public domain, CC0, CC BY 2.5 and CC BY-SA 3.0 and 4.0 terms. Source collection: aurora borealis on Wikimedia Commons.

Leaf I

Plate 01

The station, lit from inside

On the ice there is nothing to light the sky except the buildings, and so the buildings do it. A green band comes over the roof at a shallow angle and the whole curve of the station picks up its colour from underneath.

Photo by Chris Danals, National Science Foundation, Public domain

Plate 02

Corkscrew over the treeline

The classic shape, and the one that never repeats. A ray of the band rotates fast enough to draw a hook in the sky, and the line of small conifers below it is the only fixed thing in the frame.

United States Air Force photo by Senior Airman Joshua Strang, Public domain

Plate 03

A curtain with a house under it

One hut, one window lit, and a curtain that fills the entire upper half of the frame. The ground takes almost no colour at all, which is what makes the sky read as so much bigger than the land.

Rafal Konieczny, CC BY 2.5

Plate 04

Low band, high mountains

The band sits so low that the ridge cuts it in half and the snowfields either side take its light. In March the fjord is still frozen at the mouth, so nothing in the lower frame moves either.

Ximonic, CC BY-SA 3.0

Plate 05

The ice dome, lit from inside

A shelter built of ice and lit from within, so the whole thing glows the same colour as the band above it. For a few minutes in the middle of the night the ground and the sky are the same brightness.

Ross Burgener, Public domain

Plate 06

The long magenta glow

Not a curtain at all, just a wide violet wash sitting on the horizon, so bright it turns the whole lower sky pink. A single bare tree at the edge of the frame is the only thing that gives the glow a size.

Kristian Pikner, CC BY-SA 4.0

Plate 07

One watcher, still water

A person standing at the water's edge, small enough that the sky is doing all the work. The lake is flat enough to double the stars, and the figure is only readable because it blocks them.

Jamen Percy, CC BY-SA 4.0

Plate 08

Green with a pink edge

The last plate, and the clearest: oxygen green through the middle of the band with a thin nitrogen pink along the top edge where it thins. Below it the fjord is completely black and says nothing about the scale.

Johannes Groll, CC0
