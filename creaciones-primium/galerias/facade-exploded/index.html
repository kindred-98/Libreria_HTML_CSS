Facade, Exploded

Eight photographs pinned to four plates. The plates sit on the base line when the view is gathered and climb apart as the spread opens. Nothing here is a picture of a building: every plate is a drawing surface with two photographs bolted to it.

Method

Photographs are laid flat on each plate and read from the axonometric camera. Cyan wire marks the plate edge; the dashed mast drops to the base line so the depth of every plate stays legible even when the spread is closed.

Controls

Drag the view to orbit. The slider and the arrow keys set the spread. Each photograph opens full size.
