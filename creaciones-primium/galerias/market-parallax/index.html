Panorama 02 · Market walk

Four Views,
One Slow Pass

A stall front is three pictures deep: the canopy, the counter, the crowd. Scroll and the three layers part company. Four numbered views, eight plates, one printed sheet.

View one — canopy

The first layer is the roof: stripes, scallops, a rope of bulbs. It moves slowest, so the eye reads it as weather.

ABCDEFGH

View two — counter

The second layer is the merchandise, cut level with the counter. It travels at mid speed, halfway between roof and feet.

ABCDEFGH

View three — crowd

The third layer is the people in front of the counter. Fastest of the three, and deliberately soft, so faces never fight the roof.

ABCDEFGH

View four — close

By the fourth view the layers have swapped places twice. The halftone field never moves at all, which is what holds the sheet together.
