Cat. 01

Széchenyi Chain Bridge

Budapest, Hungary

The first permanent bridge over the Danube in the capital, hung from chains that were drawn in and re-drawn as traffic grew.

Builder
A. Clark and J. Perry
Main span
382 m
Opened
1849
Material
Wrought iron chain
