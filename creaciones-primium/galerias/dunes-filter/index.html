Dune register / field 07 / ten exposures

Where the sand keeps its shape

The register is a list, not a wall: one frame per line, ranked by tonal tier. Turn the dial and the surviving lines fall into two columns and re-sort, so the same photograph arrives at a different rank the moment you change the formation.
