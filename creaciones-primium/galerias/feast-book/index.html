Feast 08 · a book of eight plates

The Feast Book

Four leaves, two plates to a spread, a title page and a colophon. Click a corner of the book or use the arrow keys to turn.

Title page

This book has four leaves and no recipe in it. It is only eight photographs of food, set two to a spread, with a plate of ink behind them.

Turn the page to begin. Each leaf carries a photograph on the front and another on the back, so the spread always shows two.

—

Colophon

Eight photographs, four leaves, no text but this. The ink is claret, the paper is ivory, and everything else was left alone.

The photographs come from Wikimedia Commons under Creative Commons and public domain terms. The authors and the licences are listed at the foot of the desk.

End of book
