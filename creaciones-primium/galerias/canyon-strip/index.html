Canyon strip / traverse two / nine plates

Red stone, read left to right

The strip never stops drifting on its own. Put the pointer on it, or focus any plate, and it holds still so you can read the caption. The two anchors at the edges jump the whole traverse to its first or last plate.

Left and right walk the strip · Home and End jump to the first and last plate · Enter opens the plate · Escape closes it
