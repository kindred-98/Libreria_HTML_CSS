Ring nine / nine plates / forest green and cream

Nine birds on a soft ring

Nine field photographs cut into soft, uneven medallions and strung on a ring that drifts past on its own. The plate at the front is the one being read: it comes to full colour and full size, its name and licence settle underneath, and the rest sit back in the green. Drag the ring, use the arrows, or open the front plate in the viewer.
