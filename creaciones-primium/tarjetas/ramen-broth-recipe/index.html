Broth no. 7 · house notebook

Cloud-white pork and chicken broth

Sixteen hours at a rolling boil, then a hard skim and a long cool-down.

  • Total 16 h 20 m
  • Active 40 m
  • Yield 4 bowls
  • Level Advanced

Scale the pot every amount below follows

4bowls

Ingredients

  • Chicken feet, split

    500 g

  • Pork trotters, halved

    600 g

  • Pork neck bones

    400 g

  • Onion, halved

    2 whole

  • Garlic, crushed

    10 cloves

  • Ginger, smashed

    60 g

  • Kombu, wiped

    12 g

  • Light soy tare

    45 ml

  • Spring onion whites

    5 stalks

Technique

Simmer every bone for 20 minutes, discard the water, then scrub each piece under cold water until the knuckles show white. This single step is what keeps the finished broth clear instead of chalky.

Char the onion and ginger dry in a pan until blackened on the flat faces, then add them with the kombu and 6 litres of cold water. Bring to a full rolling boil and never let it drop below a lively bubble for the next eight hours.

Skim the foam every 20 minutes for the first three hours, more often once the liquid turns opaque. Any fat left on the surface will emulsify into a flat, greasy skin instead of a pearl-white gloss.

Once the bones give no resistance, cool the pot to 60°C overnight. Press the surface fat back into the broth in three passes, then strain twice through a fine mesh and once through linen.

Season with the tare, a pinch of MSG and nothing else. Rest for two hours so the flavour settles, then ladle at 65°C and finish each bowl with sliced pork, egg, nori and spring onion.

Hold at 65°C · consume within 24 h · pot holds 6 L at full boil
