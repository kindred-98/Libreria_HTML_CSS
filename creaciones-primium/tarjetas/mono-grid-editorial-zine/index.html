The Quiet Grid

Why a single column of type, set without decoration, still outperforms every dashboard we have shipped.

A grid is a promise you make to the reader before writing a word. It says the measure will hold, the margins will not wander, and the eye can rest on the same axis from the first line to the last.

We removed the cards, the badges and the shadows, then measured what happened to reading time. It fell by a third, and nobody wrote in to ask where the panels had gone.

Restraint, it turns out, is not a style. It is a decision about what deserves attention, made once, and then defended for twelve pages.
