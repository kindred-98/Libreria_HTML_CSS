Plate 04 · Vermillion Cut at low water

Field report · Lower delta

The long way around the delta

Nine kilometres of raised road, one tide window and a ferry that only runs when the river allows it.

RM

Rowan MbekiPhotographs and text

14 min read

The raised road stops eleven kilometres short of the water, at a gate locked since the flood. From there the only way on is a mud bank the ferryman calls the promise.

You do not cross the delta. You persuade it to let you through for one hour, at the turn of the tide.

The bank holds for forty minutes. After that it is not a road, it is a rumour.

Ivo Serrat, ferryman, Pier 6

We walked it at 05:40, with the herons lifting off the bank one at a time.
