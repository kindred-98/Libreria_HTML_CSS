Salt Almanac

i

Tide ledger

The sea keeps a ledger,

opens it at four,

stamps the sand and closes it.

Nothing owed.

2

Salt Almanac

ii

Salt on the rail

Salt climbs the handrail

all the slow night,

then the sun collects it

like a debt paid small.
