Khao Soi Gai

Northern curry noodle soup on a lamb bone reduction, finished with coconut cream and crisp wheat noodles.

  • Prep25 min
  • Simmer02:09:41
  • Yield4 bowls
  • Spice index6.4

Scale the pot

4

Heat

Ingredients base 4 bowls

  • Lamb shoulder, cubed 700 g
  • Chicken frame, split 600 g
  • Red curry paste 90 g
  • Coconut cream 400 ml
  • Fish sauce 45 ml
  • Palm sugar 18 g

Method

Cover the lamb and chicken with cold water, bring to a hard boil for eight minutes and throw the water away. Skim the scum now and the finished broth stays clear instead of chalky.

Fry the paste in the thick cream from the top of the can until the oil splits and turns orange. Add the sugar and let it catch for a minute before any liquid goes in.

Return the bones with two litres of water, hold it just under a tremble for ninety minutes and lift the clear fat off the surface every fifteen minutes.

Balance with fish sauce and a squeeze of lime, rest off the heat for twenty minutes, then ladle over crisp noodles, mustard greens and a spoonful of raw chilli oil.

Holds four bowls hot for 90 min · garnish scales 1:1
