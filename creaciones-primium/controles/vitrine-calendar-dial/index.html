Perpetual Calendar Dial

Two engraved rings, twelve months outside and thirty one days inside, read at the fixed index at the top. The small window below rolls the full date on its rollers, and the plate in front of the glass is re-stamped with whatever the dial says.

Both rings are real range inputs with their own labels: arrows step, Page keys skip, Home and End run to the ends. A date the dial cannot make is refused and the plate says so.
