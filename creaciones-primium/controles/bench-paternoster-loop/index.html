Calibration bay 2 / endless chain

Paternoster Loop

Ten reference cells hang on a chain that never comes to the end. It coasts on its own, so the value in the window keeps changing. Take a cell and the chain brakes, overshoots the window by a few millimetres and springs back into the seat, where the amber readout settles.
