Remote Volume Wheel

A wheel on the flank of the remote. Flick it and it keeps turning, the ladder answers on the face, and mute shorts the signal to silence.

Twelve segments of green follow the wheel. When mute bridges the pads the ladder drops to the floor and the display holds the last figure.

Flick the wheel or use the arrows, Page keys and Home / End. Space or Enter mutes it.
